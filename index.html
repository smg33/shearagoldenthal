How I can help

Google's automation and AI tools have changed how campaigns get built, but not what makes them work.

Since the early days of paid search, success has come down to the same fundamentals: the right keywords, ads that speak to what people are actually searching for, and landing pages that make it easy to say yes. The platforms wrap those simple ideas in layers of complexity: targeting options, audience lists, bidding strategies, campaign settings and ad assets, each with its own quirks. I know them inside and out. I've applied these fundamentals across every kind of account, from global brands and enterprise programs to startups and mom-and-pop shops, so I can set realistic goals for your size and budget. And every change I make is driven by your data, not guesswork or the platform's default recommendations.

Questions

Is my ad spend included in the fee?

No. You pay Google directly for your ads, from your own account. My fee is only for the work, so I have no reason to push your budget up.

Is there a contract?

Management is month to month. If you want to stop, give 30 days' notice. Your account, data and tracking stay yours.

How fast will I see results?

Not overnight. Google's bidding needs time to learn from your data, so new or restructured campaigns usually take 60 to 90 days to ramp up. I'll show you what's improving along the way, and we'll judge results once campaigns have had time to settle.

Why a flat fee instead of a percentage of spend?

A percentage rewards spending more, not spending well. A flat fee keeps my incentives the same as yours: better results from the money you already spend.

What if my account changes?

If your account grows in complexity, like new locations, product lines or campaign types, we'll talk about switching plans before anything changes. No surprise charges.

Do you do free consults?

Yes. A free 20-minute call to hear about your business and tell you honestly whether I can help.
